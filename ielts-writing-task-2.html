Opinion essay: to'liq namuna

Topshiriq: Some people believe that university education should be free for all students. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

In many countries the cost of higher education is rising, and some people argue that universities should be free for every student. I partly agree with this view: free education brings clear benefits to society, but it should come with fair conditions.
The main argument for free university education is equal opportunity. When tuition fees are high, talented young people from poor families may never reach university, while less able students from rich families do. Free education allows a country to use the abilities of all its citizens. Germany, where most public universities charge no tuition fees, shows that this model can work. In addition, graduates usually earn higher salaries and therefore pay more taxes, so society eventually gets its investment back.
However, completely free education also has drawbacks. Universities need large budgets for teachers, laboratories and research, and if the state pays for everything, the money must come from taxpayers who never studied there. Moreover, when something is free, some students may take it less seriously, change courses several times or fail to finish their degree.
For these reasons, I believe the best solution is a balanced system. University should be free for students who achieve good results and come from low-income families, while others could pay reasonable fees or repay loans after they start working. This would keep higher education open to talented people without placing the whole cost on the state.
In conclusion, although money should never stop able students from studying, I think free education should be linked to merit and need rather than offered to everyone without conditions.

Namunaning tahlili

  • Kirish: mavzu qayta yozilgan va pozitsiya aniq: I partly agree with this view.
  • 1-asosiy abzats: tarafdorlar dalili (teng imkoniyat), real misol (Germaniya) va iqtisodiy sabab.
  • 2-asosiy abzats: qarshi dalillar: davlat byudjeti va talabalarning mas'uliyati.
  • 3-asosiy abzats: muallifning yechimi: natija va ehtiyojga bog'langan tizim.
  • Xulosa: pozitsiya o'zgarmagan, qisqa takrorlangan. Hajm 250 so'zdan ancha ko'p.

IELTS Writing Task 1: grafik, diagramma va xat

IELTS Writing Task 1 da kamida 150 so'z yozasiz, tavsiya etilgan vaqt 20 daqiqa. Academic modulda grafik, diagramma, jadval, jarayon yoki xaritani tasvirlaysiz. General Training modulda esa xat yozasiz.

IELTS Task 1 ni tekshirish

Academic Task 1 tuzilishi

  1. Kirish: savolni o'z so'zlaringiz bilan qayta yozing (paraphrase).
  2. Overview: eng asosiy tendensiya yoki farqni 1-2 gapda ayting. Usiz Task Achievement bandi 5 dan oshishi qiyin.
  3. 2 ta asosiy abzats: ma'lumotlarni guruhlab, aniq raqamlar bilan solishtiring.

Shaxsiy fikr yozilmaydi. Platformada 48 ta Academic savol bor: line graph, bar chart, pie chart, table, process va map, har birida namuna javob.

General Training xati

40 ta xat: formal, semi-formal va informal. Har bir xatda savoldagi uchta band (bullet point) to'liq yoritilishi kerak.

Yangi alifboda qisqacha

Academic Task 1: to'liq namuna

Topshiriq: The table below shows the percentage of households with internet access in three countries in 2000, 2010 and 2020. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. (Ma'lumotlar o'quv maqsadida to'qilgan.)

Mamlakat200020102020
Country A40%75%95%
Country B15%50%88%
Country C5%20%60%

The table compares the proportion of households with internet access in three countries in 2000, 2010 and 2020.
Overall, internet access rose considerably in all three countries over the period. Country A had the highest figures throughout, while Country C, despite the most rapid relative growth, remained well behind the other two.
In 2000, 40% of households in Country A were connected to the internet, compared with only 15% in Country B and just 5% in Country C. Over the next decade, the figure for Country A almost doubled to 75%, and Country B saw an even sharper rise, reaching 50%. Country C, by contrast, grew more slowly to 20%.
Between 2010 and 2020, access continued to increase. Country A approached full coverage at 95%, and Country B climbed steeply to 88%, narrowing the gap with the leader. Meanwhile, the proportion in Country C tripled to 60%, although this was still the lowest figure in the table.

Namunaning tahlili

  • Kirish: savol qayta yozilgan: shows → compares, percentage → proportion.
  • Overview: ikki asosiy xususiyat: hamma joyda o'sish, A doim yuqorida, C orqada.
  • 1-abzats: 2000 va 2010 yillar, raqamlar solishtirish bilan (compared with, by contrast).
  • 2-abzats: 2010-2020 yillar, xilma-xil fe'llar (approached, climbed, tripled).
  • Shaxsiy fikr yo'q, faqat ma'lumot. Hajm 150 so'zdan ko'p.

Savol-javob

IELTS Task 1 necha so'z?

Kamida 150 so'z, 20 daqiqa atrofida.

Line graph va pie chart'ni qanday yozish kerak?

Kirish, overview va ikkita asosiy abzats. Line graph'da vaqt bo'yicha o'zgarishni, pie chart'da ulushlarni solishtiring.

Task 1 bandi umumiy Writing'ga qanday ta'sir qiladi?

Writing ≈ (Task 1 + 2 × Task 2) ÷ 3, ya'ni Task 2 ikki baravar muhim.

Task 1 da overview qayerda bo'ladi?

Odatda kirishdan keyin, ikkinchi abzatsda. Ba'zilar uni oxirida yozadi, asosiysi u aniq bo'lishi: eng katta tendensiya va eng muhim farq.

Hamma raqamlarni yozish kerakmi?

Yo'q. Asosiy xususiyatlarni tanlab, eng muhim raqamlar bilan solishtiring. Hamma raqamni ketma-ket sanash bahoni oshirmaydi.

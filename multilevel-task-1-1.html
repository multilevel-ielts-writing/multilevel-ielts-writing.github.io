Multilevel Task 1.1: norasmiy xat (taxminan 50 so'z)

Multilevel Task 1.1 da do'stingizga qisqa norasmiy xat yozasiz, taxminan 50 so'z. Vaziyat bitta: masalan, do'stingiz biror yangilik haqida yozgan va fikringizni so'ragan. Keyingi Task 1.2 da xuddi shu vaziyat bo'yicha rasmiy xat yoziladi.

Task 1.1 ni yozib tekshirish

Task 1.1 tuzilishi

  1. Salomlashish: Dear Ali, / Hi Madina,
  2. Munosabat: Thanks for your message. / It's great to hear from you.
  3. Asosiy javob: savolga aniq javob va bitta sabab.
  4. Yakun: Write back soon. / Best wishes, va ismingiz.

Foydali iboralar

  • I think it's a great idea because ...
  • To be honest, I'd rather ...
  • Why don't we ...?
  • Let me know what you think.

Qisqa namuna (52 so'z)

Hi Sardor,
Thanks for telling me about the new sports club. I think it's a great idea, because we both need more exercise after school. I'd love to join the football group on Saturdays. Why don't we go together next week and see the coach? Let me know!
Best wishes,
Aziz

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • Rasmiy so'zlar ishlatish (Dear Sir, I am writing to inform you): bu Task 1.2 uchun.
  • Savolga javob bermay, umumiy gap yozish.
  • 50 so'zdan ancha ko'p yozib, vaqtni Task 2 dan olish.

Yangi alifboda qisqacha

Task 1.1: döstga taxminan 50 sözli norasmiy xat. Salom, munosabat, javob va yakun. Platformada yozing, AI darhol tekşiradi.

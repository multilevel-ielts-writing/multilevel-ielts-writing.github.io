Multilevel Task 1.2: rasmiy xat (120-150 so'z)

Multilevel Task 1.2 da Task 1.1 dagi vaziyat bo'yicha rasmiy xat yozasiz: rahbar, direktor, tashkilot yoki muharrirga. Hajmi 120-150 so'z. Fikr Task 1.1 dagidek bo'lishi mumkin, lekin endi u rasmiy uslubda va to'liq tushuntiriladi.

Task 1.2 ni yozib tekshirish

Task 1.2 tuzilishi

  1. Murojaat: Dear Sir or Madam, / Dear Mr Karimov,
  2. Maqsad: I am writing to express my opinion about ...
  3. 1-2 asosiy fikr sabab va misol bilan.
  4. Taklif: I would suggest that ... / It would be helpful if ...
  5. Yakun: I look forward to your reply. Yours faithfully (ism noma'lum bo'lsa) yoki Yours sincerely (ism ma'lum bo'lsa).

Rasmiy uslub uchun maslahatlar

  • Qisqartmalarsiz yozing: I'm emas, I am.
  • Kuchliroq so'zlar: get emas receive, help emas assist.
  • Bog'lovchilar: Firstly, In addition, However, Therefore.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • Task 1.1 dagi norasmiy iboralarni ko'chirish (Hi, Thanks, See you).
  • Maqsadni birinchi gapda aytmaslik.
  • Yakunda Yours faithfully va Yours sincerely ni adashtirish.

Yangi alifboda qisqacha

Task 1.2: 120-150 sözli rasmiy xat. Maqsad, sabablar, taklif va rasmiy yakun. Platformada yozing, AI xatolarni özbekça tuşuntiradi.

To'liq namuna (138 so'z)

Vaziyat: Shahringizda yangi kutubxona ochilmoqchi. Do'stingiz Kamola bu haqda sizga xat yozgan. Shahar hokimligi esa aholidan yangi kutubxona qanday bo'lishi haqida fikr so'ramoqda. Task 1.2 da hokimlikka yoki kutubxona rahbariga rasmiy xat yozasiz.

Dear Sir or Madam,
I am writing to share my views on the plan to open a new library in our district. As a student who uses the current library every week, I strongly support this idea.
Firstly, the existing building is too small for the number of visitors. In the evenings, it is almost impossible to find a free seat, so many students have to study at home in noisy conditions. Secondly, the collection of English books is very limited and mostly outdated.
I would therefore suggest including a quiet study area and a modern foreign language section in the new library. It would also be helpful if it stayed open until 9 p.m. on weekdays.
Thank you for considering my suggestions. I look forward to hearing about the progress of the project.
Yours faithfully,
Dilnoza Rahimova

Namunaning tahlili

  • 1-abzats: xat maqsadi birinchi gapda (I am writing to share my views on...) va yozuvchi kimligi.
  • 2-abzats: ikki sabab, Firstly va Secondly bilan, har biri tushuntirilgan.
  • 3-abzats: aniq takliflar: I would therefore suggest..., It would also be helpful if...
  • Yakun: minnatdorchilik va rasmiy xayrlashish. Murojaat Dear Sir or Madam bo'lgani uchun Yours faithfully.
  • Uslub: qisqartmalar yo'q, so'zlar rasmiy: existing, collection, considering.

# Yozgin AI: IELTS and Multilevel (CEFR) Writing checker

**Yozgin AI** is a free web platform that checks English writing for the Uzbekistan Multilevel (CEFR) exam and for IELTS. The AI gives a score by the exam criteria and explains every mistake in Uzbek.

👉 **Open the platform: https://multilevel-ielts-writing.github.io/**

## What is inside

- **Multilevel (CEFR) Writing:** Task 1.1, Task 1.2 and Task 2 in the new format, with real exam questions
- **IELTS Writing:** Task 1 (Academic and General Training) and Task 2, with band scores by the 4 criteria
- **53 lessons from A1 to C1**, a placement test and **38 timed mock tests**
- **Photo check:** take a picture of a handwritten text and get it checked
- Daily streaks, rewards and a results list

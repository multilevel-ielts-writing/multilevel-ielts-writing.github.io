Qanday maʼlumot olinadi

Röyxatdan ötişda ismingiz, familiyangiz, emailingiz va tanlagan imtihoningiz (CEFR yoki IELTS) saqlanadi. Natijalar röyxati uçun eng yaxşi natijangiz, XP va neça kun ketma-ket kirganingiz saqlanadi.

Matnlaringiz va rasmlaringiz

Tekşirişga yuborgan matningiz va rasmlaringiz AI baholaşi uçun yuboriladi. AI tekşiruvini Anthropic kompaniyasining Claude modeli bajaradi. Matn faqat baholaş uçun yuboriladi. Rasmlar saqlanmaydi. Yozgan matnlaringiz, qoralamalaringiz va darslardagi natijalaringiz qurilmangizda saqlanadi. Röyxatdan ötgan bölsangiz, ularning zaxira nusxasi serverda ham saqlanadi: telefoningizni almaştirsangiz, progress qaytib keladi. Zaxirani faqat siz oça olasiz: buning uçun Sozlama böyimidagi tiklaş kodi kerak.

Texnik xabarlar

Ilovada xato çiqsa, xato matni, sahifa nomi va brauzer turi muallifga avtomatik yuboriladi. Bu xatolarni tez tuzatiş uçun kerak. Unda ismingiz yoki matningiz bölmaydi.

Kim köradi

Natijalar röyxatida faqat ismingiz, familiyangizning birinçi harfi va natijangiz körinadi. Röyxatda körinişni xohlamasangiz, Sozlama böyimida belgini olib taşlang. Emailingiz va töliq ismingizni faqat platforma muallifi köradi. Maʼlumotlar boşqalarga berilmaydi, sotilmaydi, reklama yuborilmaydi.

Saqlaş va öçiriş

Maʼlumotlar Cloudflare serverida saqlanadi. Sozlama böyimidagi "Hisobni öçiriş" tugmasi serverdagi barça maʼlumotlaringizni, zaxira nusxasi bilan birga, öçiradi. Yordam kerak bölsa, quyidagi emailga yozing, 7 kun içida öçiriladi.

Yoş

Platformadan maktab öquvçilari ham foydalanişi mumkin. AI faqat yozilgan matn haqida izoh beradi va bu izohni AI yozgani har doim körsatiladi. 13 yoşdan kiçik bolalar ota-onasi ruxsati bilan foydalanişi kerak.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you sign up we store your first name, last name, email address and chosen exam (CEFR or IELTS). For the public results list we store your best result, your XP points and your daily streak.

Your texts and photos

Texts and photos you send for checking are sent to an AI service to assess them. The checks are done by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Your text is sent only to be assessed. Photos are not stored. Your texts, drafts and lesson progress are stored on your device. If you have an account, a backup copy is also stored on our server so that you can continue on a new phone. Only you can restore it, with the recovery code shown in Settings.

Technical reports

If an error happens in the app, the error text, the page name and the browser type are sent to the author automatically, so that errors can be fixed quickly. They do not include your name or your texts.

Who can see your data

The public results list shows only your first name, the first letter of your last name and your result. You can hide yourself from the list in Settings. Only the author of the platform can see your email and full name. We do not sell or share your data and we do not send advertising.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored on Cloudflare servers. The "Delete account" button in Settings deletes all your data from the server, including the backup. You can also email us and we will delete it within 7 days.

Age

School students can use the platform too. The AI only gives feedback on the text you wrote, and the app always shows that the feedback comes from an AI. Children under 13 should use it with a parent's permission.

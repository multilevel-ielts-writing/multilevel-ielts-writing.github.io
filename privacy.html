Qanday maʼlumot olinadi

Röyxatdan ötişda ismingiz, familiyangiz, emailingiz va tanlagan imtihoningiz (CEFR yoki IELTS) saqlanadi. Natijalar röyxati uçun eng yaxşi natijangiz, XP va neça kun ketma-ket kirganingiz saqlanadi.

Matnlaringiz va rasmlaringiz

Tekşirişga yuborgan matningiz va rasmlaringiz AI baholaşi uçun yuboriladi. AI tekşiruvini Anthropic kompaniyasining Claude modeli bajaradi. Matn faqat baholaş uçun yuboriladi. Rasmlar saqlanmaydi. Yozgan matnlaringiz, qoralamalaringiz va darslardagi natijalaringiz qurilmangizda saqlanadi. Röyxatdan ötgan bölsangiz, ularning zaxira nusxasi serverda ham saqlanadi: telefoningizni almaştirsangiz, progress qaytib keladi. Yangi qurilmada emailingizni yozib kirsangiz, zaxira avtomatik qaytadi. Şuning uçun emailingizni begona odamlarga bermang.

Tadqiqot

Platforma muallifning ilmiy tadqiqoti doirasida yaratilgan. Tadqiqot uçun tekşirilgan har bir matnning faqat raqamlari saqlanadi: ball, mezonlar böyiça baho, sözlar soni va sana. Platformani qaysi kunlari oçganingiz ham saqlanadi: «ketma-ket kun» hisobi va faollikni örganiş uçun. Bu raqamlarni faqat muallif köradi. Ular öquvçilarning boşlanğiç darajasi va ösişini tahlil qiliş uçun işlatiladi. Matnlaringizning özi tadqiqot jadvaliga kiritilmaydi.

Eslatmalar va Telegram

Eslatma faqat siz rozi bölsangiz keladi: telefon yoki kompyuterdagi bildirişnoma yoki Telegram orqali, soat 12:00 da (Toşkent vaqti), platformani oçmagan kuningiz. 14 kungaça har kuni, keyin har 3 kunda keladi, 60 kundan keyin töxtaydi. Bildirişnoma uçun serverda faqat brauzeringiz bergan bildirişnoma manzili saqlanadi. Telegram uçun faqat suhbat raqami saqlanadi: telefon raqamingiz va Telegram ismingiz olinmaydi. Imtihon sanasini kiritsangiz, eslatmada «Imtihongaça N kun» yoziladi. Eslatmalarni Sozlama böyimida yoki Telegram'da /stop yozib istalgan payt öçiriş mumkin. Hisobni öçirsangiz, bu maʼlumotlar ham öçadi.

Platformaning Telegram kanalida (@yozgin_ai) har kuni darslardan bitta test savoli çiqadi. Bu savollarga javoblar anonim: kim qanday javob bergani saqlanmaydi.

Texnik xabarlar

Ilovada xato çiqsa, xato matni, sahifa nomi va brauzer turi muallifga avtomatik yuboriladi. Bu xatolarni tez tuzatiş uçun kerak. Unda ismingiz yoki matningiz bölmaydi.

Kim köradi

Natijalar röyxatida faqat ismingiz, familiyangizning birinçi harfi va natijangiz körinadi. Röyxatda barça öquvçilar şu körinişda turadi. Emailingiz va töliq ismingizni faqat platforma muallifi köradi. Maʼlumotlar boşqalarga berilmaydi, sotilmaydi, reklama yuborilmaydi.

Saqlaş va öçiriş

Maʼlumotlar Cloudflare serverida saqlanadi. Sozlama böyimidagi "Hisobni öçiriş" tugmasi serverdagi barça maʼlumotlaringizni, zaxira nusxasi bilan birga, öçiradi. Yordam kerak bölsa, quyidagi emailga yozing, 7 kun içida öçiriladi.

Yoş

Platformadan maktab öquvçilari ham foydalanişi mumkin. AI faqat yozilgan matn haqida izoh beradi va bu izohni AI yozgani har doim körsatiladi. 13 yoşdan kiçik bolalar ota-onasi ruxsati bilan foydalanişi kerak.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you sign up we store your first name, last name, email address and chosen exam (CEFR or IELTS). For the public results list we store your best result, your XP points and your daily streak.

Your texts and photos

Texts and photos you send for checking are sent to an AI service to assess them. The checks are done by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Your text is sent only to be assessed. Photos are not stored. Your texts, drafts and lesson progress are stored on your device. If you have an account, a backup copy is also stored on our server so that you can continue on a new phone. When you sign in on a new device with your email, the backup comes back automatically, so please do not share your email with strangers.

Research

The platform was created as part of the author's academic research. For this research only the numbers of each checked text are stored: the score, the marks for each criterion, the number of words and the date. The days on which you open the platform are also stored, for the daily streak and to study how often learners practise. Only the author can see these numbers. They are used to analyse learners' starting level and progress. Your texts themselves are not added to the research table.

Reminders and Telegram

Reminders come only if you agree: as a notification on your phone or computer, or in Telegram, at 12:00 Tashkent time on a day you have not opened the platform. They come daily for 14 days, then every 3 days, and stop after 60 days. For notifications the server stores only the notification address that your browser gives. For Telegram it stores only the chat number: not your phone number and not your Telegram name. If you enter your exam date, the reminder says how many days are left. You can turn reminders off in Settings or by sending /stop in Telegram. Deleting your account deletes this data too.

The platform's Telegram channel (@yozgin_ai) posts one quiz question from the lessons every day. The answers are anonymous: nobody's answers are stored.

Technical reports

If an error happens in the app, the error text, the page name and the browser type are sent to the author automatically, so that errors can be fixed quickly. They do not include your name or your texts.

Who can see your data

The public results list shows only your first name, the first letter of your last name and your result. All learners appear in the list in this form. Only the author of the platform can see your email and full name. We do not sell or share your data and we do not send advertising.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored on Cloudflare servers. The "Delete account" button in Settings deletes all your data from the server, including the backup. You can also email us and we will delete it within 7 days.

Age

School students can use the platform too. The AI only gives feedback on the text you wrote, and the app always shows that the feedback comes from an AI. Children under 13 should use it with a parent's permission.
